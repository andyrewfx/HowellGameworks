# Howell Gameworks Studio - Public Product Roadmap

This roadmap summarizes progress on the Howell Gameworks Studio creative, omnichannel marketing, and production workflows. It is separate from other Howell Gameworks projects.

## Progress snapshot

| Area | Status | Public summary |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Creative studio and product presentation | Built and evolving | Workflows for shaping and presenting collectible products across merchandise formats. |
| Drop planning and promotion | Built and demonstrated | A multi-channel release workspace, channel selection, and promotional-sheet creation. |
| Community commerce experiences | Demonstrated | Product browsing and presentation workflows for community channels. |
| Print preparation | Core workflows in place | Tools for organizing product selections and preparing print output. |
| Marketplace connections | In progress | Integration work is underway; channel-by-channel readiness varies and is not being announced as generally available. |
| Cross-channel reporting | Planned | Future exploration of clearer activity and production visibility. |

For previous detailed phase notes, consult the [historical progress archive](./PUBLIC_PROGRESS_ARCHIVE.md). For customer-facing direction, see the [omnichannel marketing roadmap](./PUBLIC_OMNICHANNEL_MARKETING_ROADMAP.md).

## Current stage

This product has **not yet entered a customer pilot**. The statuses above describe development progress and demonstrations, not customer deployments or general availability. Scope and priorities may change; this roadmap makes no delivery-date commitments.

## Public scope

This page describes product capabilities at a high level. It intentionally excludes source code, internal architecture, private operating details, and customer data.
