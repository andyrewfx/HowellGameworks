# Howell Gameworks - Public Omnichannel Marketing Roadmap

**Current roadmap — October 30, 2026**

This is the public view of how Howell Gameworks plans to connect the creator, collector, community, and commerce sides of its TCG/CCG products. It is a staged direction, not a launch calendar or a claim that every integration is complete.

TCG Battleship provides the creator-to-production workflow. Novus TCG is a distinct collector and community product. The long-term opportunity is to connect discovery, releases, community activity, sales, and production where the products and reliable data make that useful.

## Foundation already delivered

The active TCG Battleship roadmap marks its first twelve delivery phases complete or implemented. That work established the creative studio, multi-format product preparation, print and stream-fulfillment workflows, the custom Discord shop, audience-aware content protections, and channel-aware drop management.

Discord/VOD POD sales and Etsy sales are live. Whatnot stream and post-show report workflows are in use. The first shared analytics slice brings recorded Whatnot and Discord/VOD POD activity together. The [product progress report](./PUBLIC_PRODUCT_PROGRESS.md) preserves the delivered milestones; the [channel status page](./PUBLIC_CHANNEL_STATUS_AND_ANALYTICS.md) defines exactly what each integration supports today.

## Prioritized next outcomes

| Priority | Workstream | Current boundary | Intended next outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Etsy order-to-production** | Sales and seller-side listing management are live/implemented; real receipt reconciliation into print fulfillment is not verified. | Validate actual receipt data and complete a reliable, reviewable order handoff before describing the API path as operational. |
| **2** | **eBay connection** | Initial groundwork only; no live seller/listing or sales workflow. | Validate seller connection, order requirements, and listing readiness before enabling a live channel. |
| **3** | **Cross-channel sales analytics** | Whatnot and Discord/VOD POD are the initial connected sources; other sources are incomplete. | Extend attribution as channel order data becomes verified, retaining visibility into missing and ambiguous records. |
| **4** | **Production and print reporting** | Orders, fulfillment states, and physical print events are not interchangeable. Durable actual-print reporting is incomplete. | Record and report sold units, giveaways, fulfillment, and actual physical copies as distinct measures. |
| **5** | **Marketing and engagement insight** | Purchase records do not reveal all audience exposure, viewer engagement, or conversion denominators. | Instrument useful channel engagement and relate it to sales only where evidence is available and comparable. |
| **6** | **Novus TCG and creator ecosystem** | Novus TCG and TCG Battleship are separate product tracks with complementary roles. | Continue product development and make selected community, inventory, and commerce journeys work together without obscuring product boundaries. |

The order expresses current delivery focus, not fixed dates. Existing Discord and Etsy sales workflows are production-critical and should not be disrupted by new channel work.

## How the marketing loop is intended to grow

1. **Create and prepare:** build card catalogs and product presentations in TCG Battleship.
2. **Plan and promote:** prepare drops and channel-appropriate previews.
3. **Sell in the right context:** support direct Discord commerce, live-stream workflows, and selected marketplace channels as each is validated.
4. **Connect collectors:** use Novus TCG's collection, discovery, social, alert, and streamer experiences to support collector engagement.
5. **Learn from evidence:** compare recorded sales, customer interest, channel activity, and production only when the underlying events are reliably captured.

The strategy is omnichannel, but that does not mean every channel shares the same storefront, policy, order lifecycle, or readiness status.

## Explorations, not commitments

- **Whatnot live-auction companion:** proposed for operator-confirmed auction capture and stream controls; discovery and implementation are not scheduled. Existing post-show Show Report import is separate and already in use.
- **Card sizzle-reel creator:** exploratory creative-marketing idea, not an implemented workflow.
- **Additional marketplaces and social channels:** candidates for later evaluation, not committed launches.
- **Broader audience-to-sale attribution:** a longer-term goal dependent on trustworthy exposure and engagement data; not available as a complete measurement today.

## Public commitments and stage

Howell Gameworks is founder-led. The company has **not yet run external customer pilots**. This roadmap describes priorities and desired outcomes without promising dates, claiming market validation, or exposing internal implementation details.
