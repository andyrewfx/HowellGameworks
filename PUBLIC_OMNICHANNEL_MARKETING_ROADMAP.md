# Howell Gameworks - Omnichannel TCG Ecosystem Roadmap

**Current roadmap — October 30, 2026**

Howell Gameworks is building an end-to-end TCG/CCG ecosystem: TCG Battleship helps creators develop, promote, sell, and produce card products; Novus TCG helps collectors organize, discover, and share collections, while supporting streamer and community experiences.

The goal is to connect product creation with the full life of a card product: creative development, release promotion, live and passive sales, collector engagement, analytics, and physical fulfillment. This is a founder-led product vision being delivered in stages—not a claim that every channel or shared workflow is complete today.

## Progress delivered

### 1. Card creation and production foundation — operational

TCG Battleship supports card catalog creation and management, creative editing, multiple product presentations, print preparation, and print/fulfillment workflows. Stream activity can feed selected cards into the print process.

### 2. Live creator and direct community commerce — operational

Live stream games and overlays support creator-led shows. A custom Discord sales channel connects product discovery, carts, checkout, and print-on-demand orders directly with the platform. Drop planning and promotional tools support releases across selected channels.

### 3. Novus TCG collection and community product — operating and evolving

Novus TCG provides a standalone AI-driven collection, folder, portfolio, and social experience, with local release alerts, Discord connections, streamer tools, games, leaderboards, and live inventory/sales integration.

### 4. Initial channel sales and analytics — operating, with integration gaps

- **Discord:** direct sales and print-on-demand order workflows are live.
- **Whatnot:** live stream workflows and post-show Show Report purchase capture are in use; captured activity feeds the initial sales analytics and fulfillment workflows.
- **Etsy:** sales are live and seller-side listing management tooling is implemented and being validated. Automated receipt/order handoff to print fulfillment remains unverified.
- **eBay:** integration groundwork is underway; marketplace sales and listing workflows are not live.

The detailed [product progress report](./PUBLIC_PRODUCT_PROGRESS.md) explains what each status means and where the current analytics limits are.

## Analytics vision

The long-term goal is business-focused analytics across Whatnot live selling, custom Discord commerce, Etsy, eBay, and other channels that may be selected later. This includes understanding which products and releases resonate, how customer activity differs between live and passive sales, and how commercial demand relates to production.

The first implemented analytics slice covers Whatnot Show Report purchases and paid Discord/VOD POD order activity. The broader goal—complete cross-channel revenue and customer-engagement attribution, channel conversion analysis, and reliable actual-print reporting—is still being developed. Sales records alone do not measure exposure, viewer engagement, or conversion, and the product will not present those as known when source data is missing.

## Explorations, not commitments

- A Whatnot live-auction companion for operator-confirmed capture and stream controls is proposed but not scheduled. Existing Show Report import is separate and already in use.
- Additional creative marketing tools, such as a card sizzle-reel workflow, are exploratory.
- Future channel additions depend on product priorities, platform permissions, and integration readiness.

## Founder-led execution

This is an ambitious ecosystem being developed by one founder. The roadmap prioritizes completing and validating useful workflows step by step, while protecting existing live sales operations. There are **no external customer pilots yet**, and no dates or channel launch promises are implied.
