# Novus TCG

**A collector-first TCG platform for organizing, discovering, and sharing the hobby.**

Novus TCG is a self-contained collector and community product within the broader Howell Gameworks ecosystem. Where TCG Battleship helps creators develop and sell card products, Novus TCG focuses on the people who collect, follow, and play around them.

## Collector and community experience

Novus TCG brings together:

- AI-assisted collection discovery and organization.
- Collection folders and portfolio views.
- Social features for sharing collections and participating in a collector community.
- Local alerts for trading-card releases and drops.
- Discord-connected collector and community experiences.

## Streamer and commerce connections

The product direction also includes streamer-integrated games and tools, leaderboard experiences, and links between live product inventory and sales. These capabilities are intended to connect the collector experience with creators and card products without making the two products one application.

## Part of a larger ecosystem

Novus TCG and TCG Battleship have distinct roles and user journeys. The broader direction is to make useful creator-to-collector connections across community, product discovery, inventory, and commerce while keeping each product understandable on its own.

Novus TCG is being developed as part of a founder-led effort. Howell Gameworks has not yet run external customer pilots.
