# TCG Battleship

**A creator-to-commerce-to-production platform for trading and collectible card products.**

TCG Battleship brings card-set development, creative production, promotion, sales, streaming workflows, and print fulfillment into one operating environment. It is designed to help a small creator or business manage more of a card product's lifecycle without stitching every step together by hand.

## What it supports

### Build and prepare products

- Create and manage card sets and catalogs in a dedicated studio.
- Edit card artwork, layout, metadata, and presentation.
- Prepare products for trading cards, fine-art prints, and stickers using format-aware previews and print workflows.
- Review a set and organize print-ready sheets, including paired card fronts and backs for supported stream jobs.

### Promote and sell

- Plan product drops and prepare channel-aware promotional material.
- Run a custom Discord shop connected to product discovery, carts, checkout, and print-on-demand order handling.
- Operate streamer-facing games, card queues, overlays, and winner displays.
- Import Whatnot Show Reports after a show and connect matched purchases to stream activity and fulfillment workflows.
- Sell through Etsy; seller-side listing workflows are connected to the broader toolset, while API-driven order intake and production handoff remain under validation.

### Carry sales into production

- Organize stream wins and other selected work into print and fulfillment workflows.
- Preserve card and run context through supported print-package exports.
- Review sales activity across the channels and source records currently connected to analytics.

These are connected capabilities, not a claim that every external marketplace has a finished, end-to-end API integration. The [channel status and analytics page](./PUBLIC_CHANNEL_STATUS_AND_ANALYTICS.md) calls out the current boundaries.

## Product boundaries and current stage

TCG Battleship is the creator and operator product in the Howell Gameworks ecosystem. **Novus TCG** is a distinct collector and community product; the two are complementary, not different names for the same application.

The core Battleship workflow is operational. Discord commerce and Etsy sales are live, and Whatnot stream/report workflows are in use. Etsy's API order-to-production loop is not yet verified, and eBay selling/listing workflows are not live. The company has not yet run an external customer pilot.
